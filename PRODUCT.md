## Product Purpose

Rivanta Green Innovative Venture Private Limited is preparing a professional company website describing its industrial development direction. The user requires a premium, realistic, investment-ready presentation.

The local homepage contains a cinematic campus journey in Silicon, CBG, SAF and Power order. The venture is new and facilities are proposed. Preserve the existing journey, native scrolling outside it, keyboard controls, reduced-motion and no-JavaScript alternatives.

Do not show acreage in public copy. Do not invent operating plants, achievements, capacities, certifications, partnerships, approvals or financial results. New work is local; deployment is not authorized.
